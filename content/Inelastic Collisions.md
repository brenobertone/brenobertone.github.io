# Inelastic Collisions
Collision in which kinetic energy is lost.

The coefficient of restitution is defined as:
$$
\varepsilon = \frac{|v_2 - v_1|}{|u_2-u_1|}
$$
In a totally inelastic collision, $\varepsilon=0$, while for an [[Elastic Collisions of Two Particles]], it it 1.
