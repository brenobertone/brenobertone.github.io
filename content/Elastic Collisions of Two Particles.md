# Elastic Collisions of Two Particles
Collision in which the total kinetic energy is conserved

Using two coordinate systems: 
- **CM**, for the center of mass
- **LAB**, for the observer at rest

Let:
	$m_1$ = mass of moving particle
	$m_2$ = mass of particle in rest at **LAB**
	$u/u'$ = initial velocity in **LAB/CM**
	$v/v'$ = final velocity in **LAB/CM**
	$T_0/T_0'$ = total kinetic energy in **LAB/CM**	
	$T_1/T_1'$ = final kinetic energy of $m_1$ in **LAB/CM**	
	$V$ = velocity of the center of mass in **LAB**
	$\psi$ = $m_1$'s deflection angle in **LAB**
	$\xi$ = $m_2$'s deflection angle in **LAB**
	$\theta$ = $m_1$ and $m_2$'s deflection angle in **CM**
