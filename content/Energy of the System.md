# Energy of the System
The total kinetic energy is the sum of the kinetic energy of the center of mass and the kinetic energy of the individual particles relative to the center of mass

$$E = \sum_\alpha \frac{m_\alpha v'^2_\alpha}{2} + \frac{MV^2}{2}$$

The total energy in a conservative system is constant
