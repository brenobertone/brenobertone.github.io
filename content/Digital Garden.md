# Digital Garden
"In order to be interesting you need to be interested"

Shallow content consumption is a trap that wastes my time, drains my energy, and feeds big tech companies with my potential.  
In today’s internet, it’s easy to fall into endless doomscrolling.  
I want to learn and grow — I have the world’s knowledge at my fingertips, yet it slips through my mind without taking root.

A digital garden seems to be a way to go against the flow.

Thinking while consuming.
Having intention.
Making connections.

Benefits to it: 
- Slow down: Learning cannot be rushed
- Feedback: If you cannot write something in your own words, you do not understand it (Feynman Technique)
- Zettelkasten: Connections between knowledge
- Actionability: Notes become an indirect measure of learning, and are easily turned into tasks
