# Life is a Video Game, Here are the Cheat Codes
1. What the game is
2. How to play it
3. Why I have failed before

Life is an infinite game: the goal is to play as long and meaningfully as possible
Character building: failures advance my character

Quests: I design the quests. The goal is to design it to my character grows.
**CHEAT CODE**: Avoid side-quests. Be focused on the main quests. 

Step 1: Self knowledge. Find out what your character ir.
**CHEAT CODE:** Go to therapy. 

Step 2: Choose the main-quests. They should reveal your character and promote growth.
**CHEAT CODE**: Set boundaries. Do not be swayed by others.

Step 3: Focused improvement. Growth comes from losing hard battles
**CHEAT CODE:** Learn to enjoy failure. 

Step 4: Find allies and enemies. 
**CHEAT CODE:** Helping others will advance your quest.
