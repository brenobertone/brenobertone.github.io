# Center of Mass
For a discrete set of particles, we define center of mass as:
$$R = \frac{1}{M}\sum_{i=0}^n m_nr_n$$
where $r_n$ is the vector connecting the origin to the particle and $M=\sum_{i=0}^n m_n$

For a continuous distribution, we use an integral:
$$R = \frac{1}{M}  \int r dm$$
